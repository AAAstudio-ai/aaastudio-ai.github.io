The second brain, explained

A second brain is a folder of text files, on your computer, that explain each side of your company: your clients, your prices, how you sell and how you write. The AI reads them before every job. The AI changes every month; this folder stays yours, and stays valid.

Once it is in place, this is what it looks like.
