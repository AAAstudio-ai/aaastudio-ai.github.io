Your questions

Before you call us

You keep what works. Your email and business software stay in place, and only your Excel tracking files change.

  • These files are imported into a database of your own, with their history. Your team then works from a single version, instead of one copy per computer.
  • The portal reads this database live: everyone sees up-to-date figures, according to the access you give them, without asking anyone or sending files around.
  • Figures from your business software show up on the same screen as everything else, through a direct connection if the software offers one, or each time you export them if it doesn't.

We write it with you, based on what you tell us. You don't need to prepare anything.

  • During the free 30-minute call, you describe the task that's taking up your time, in your own words. That's our starting point.
  • If needed, we then observe how your team really works: who does what, in which tool, at what point information gets copied over.
  • Then you get a written proposal with no technical terms. You review it and tell us what to change. We start building once you approve it.

We build the system for exactly that: it gets its data from your tools, instead of adding more data entry.

  • What used to be copied from one file to another is entered once, then appears everywhere it's needed.
  • We start from what your team already does: the screens follow the steps your team takes and the words it uses.
  • We help your team get up to speed and we solve one problem at a time, such as tracking your quotes, which you approve before the next.

It stays in your accounts. The database and hosting are set up in your name from the start, and you remain the owner.

  • You decide who gets in. You're the portal's administrator: you invite each person by email, choose their role (administrator, member, read-only) and can remove access at any time.
  • Our access depends on you, just like your team's.
  • If we use an outside service, such as a writing tool, we name it in writing with its data protection settings, and you approve it before we start. We'll sign a confidentiality agreement if you'd like.

The system belongs to you. What happens next is up to you.

  • It keeps running without us: your data and portal are already in your accounts. Hosting and outside services are billed to you directly, and their cost is set out in writing before we start.
  • If you'd like, we'll hand over the code, no strings attached.
  • If your company changes, we adapt the system, at a price agreed in writing before we start. Monthly support is available on request, with its scope agreed in writing.
