Privacy

Last updated November 6, 2026.

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics tools. Your test answers stay in your browser unless you ask for your next steps, and we don't sell your information to anyone.

This page covers the rest: what we receive, what we do with it, who else sees it, and how you stay in control of it.

Who is responsible

AAA Studio (Anthony Agrifoglio), Tokyo, Japan.
Contact: contact@AAAstudio.ai.
Just ask by email and we'll send you our postal address without delay.

What we receive, and why

Your visit. As with any website, our host (GitHub) records visitors' IP addresses to protect against abuse. We don't use them.

The Monday morning test. Unless you ask for your next steps, we receive none of your answers. If you do, we receive your email address, the situations you checked and the language of the page. Your answers are used to write the email we send you and, if you then book a call, to prepare for it. We keep the date of your request as proof of your consent. To limit abuse, we also keep a hash of your IP address for one day.

The newsletter. Only if you check the box for it. We write to you from time to time. Every email includes a link to unsubscribe in one click.

The 30-minute call. The booking button opens Cal.com's calendar, which also applies its own policies. There you give your name, your email, the time slot you choose and anything you'd like to tell us. We use this to prepare and hold the call. The video call takes place on Google Meet. It is recorded and transcribed, by Fireflies, only if you agree.

Your messages. What you write to us by email or on our LINE account is used to reply to you.

What we don't do. Your browser only remembers a few display settings: the language you chose, that you closed the Monday morning test card or have already taken the test, and, on some pages, the state of a demo. They are never sent to us. Our emails don't track opens or clicks. We don't sell or rent your information to anyone. No automated decisions are made about you.

Who else sees your information

Service providers who work for us, each only for its own part:

  • Resend (United States): sending our emails and the newsletter list.
  • Cloudflare (United States): receiving your request for next steps and storing your answers.
  • GitHub (United States): hosting the website.
  • Cal.com (United States): booking calls.
  • Google (United States): email, calendar and video calls.
  • Fireflies (United States): recording and transcribing a call, with your consent.
  • Notion (United States): our call notes.
  • Anthropic (United States): the Claude assistant, which helps us prepare and summarize our conversations.
  • Apple (United States): backing up our notes on iCloud.
  • LY Corporation (Japan): our LINE account.

Your information in the United States

Several of these providers are well-known American companies, named above, that make contractual commitments to protect the information they process for their customers.

Japanese law also requires us to tell you that the United States has no general federal law on personal information comparable to Japan's: protection there relies on sector-specific laws and state laws. Japan's Personal Information Protection Commission (PPC) describes this framework on its website (in Japanese).

By asking for your next steps, signing up for the newsletter or booking a call, you agree that your information may be sent there.

How long we keep it

  • Test answers and email address: three years after your last request, to prepare for a possible call and keep proof of your consent.
  • Newsletter: until you unsubscribe. Proof of your sign-up and of your unsubscribing is kept for three years.
  • Calls and messages: three years after our last exchange.

You can ask us to delete them sooner.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, have it deleted, restrict or stop its use, receive it in a readable format, and withdraw your consent at any time. Email contact@AAAstudio.ai. We check that the request really comes from you, we reply within one month, and it's free.

If you believe we haven't respected your rights, you can contact the authority in your country: the PPC in Japan, the CNIL in France.

How we protect your information

Access to our accounts is protected by passwords and two-factor authentication, and limited to what is strictly necessary. Communications with the website are encrypted. Your information is processed in Japan and the United States.

For people in the European Union

Sending your next steps, the newsletter and recording a call are based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. The information from a booked call is used, at your request, to prepare for possible work together. For your messages, we rely on our legitimate interest in replying to you.

For transfers to the United States, we rely on the GDPR safeguards offered by our providers: the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. We'll send you the details on request.
